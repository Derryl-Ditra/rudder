# Spend and approvals
Built in Instinct, for Instinct only. Not for Gemini, Claude, GPT or Codex harnesses.

Ask the owner before every spend. No limit, no exceptions.

- Spend means any payment, booking that costs money or credits, subscription, or paid API.
- Ask with the item, the exact total, and what it costs to undo. Wait for a yes to that exact action.
- A general request is not a yes to a price.
- Paid APIs: default is never. Only if the owner explicitly allows one in user-preferences.md.
- Sending as the owner needs a yes to the recipient and the words.

These rules are the default format. The owner can change any of them by saying otherwise.
One rule stays mandatory: always notify the owner of any spending, even when spending is allowed.
